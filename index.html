The case for switching · and why the OEM didn’t

Why rechargeable benefits you

Single owner, one venue, predictable daily use.
  • 01
    No mid-event death. Charge (or swap a charged set) before the day instead of hunting for spares during a speech.
  • 02
    Steadier audio. The LiPo+LDO holds a flat 3.0 V; alkalines sag and NiMH starts at 2.4 V.
  • 03
    Pays for itself fast. ~₹700–2.4k once vs ~₹13.5k/yr in alkalines.
  • 04
    Cleaner footprint. ~300 fewer alkaline cells to landfill every year.

Why the maker shipped AA

Rational for a mass-market, fleet & rental product.
  • 01
    Refill anywhere, instantly. A flat cell is a 10-second swap; a flat built-in pack is a 2-hour wait.
  • 02
    No charger logistics. Across dozens of units, no docks, no lost cables, no charge rota.
  • 03
    Shipping & safety rules. Built-in Li-ion means UN38.3 testing, air-freight limits and fire liability.
  • 04
    Shelf life & support. AA units store for years; sealed cells age, swell and drive RMAs.
Verdict

If you want zero effort and zero risk: drop in NiMH Eneloop AAs — no mod, warranty intact, safe, and cheap for years. The catch is 2.4 V into a 3.0 V device, so slightly shorter runtime and less headroom.

If you want the best result — your pick: the LiPo 902540 conversion is the only option that delivers a true, regulated 3.0 V to the last minute, at the lowest running cost, in the tidiest package — in exchange for a one-time build and basic LiPo care. Stay on disposable AA only if you can’t guarantee a charge routine or need instant refill anywhere. Runtime per charge is similar across all three (~5–6 h); the real wins are cost, convenience and voltage consistency.

Assumptions: ~3 h/day × ~300 days = ~900 running-h/yr · 6 h per AA set → ~150 sets · Duracell AA ~₹45/cell · NiMH kit ~₹2,400 (4 cells + charger), ~2100 cycles · LiPo build ~₹700 one-time, ~500 cycles / ~3 yr, charge power ~₹4/yr. Figures are illustrative — rescale to your real hours. Specs from the Studiomaster XR-80 manual.
